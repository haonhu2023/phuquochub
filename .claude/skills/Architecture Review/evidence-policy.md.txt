# Purpose

This document defines

the official Evidence Policy

for the PhuQuocHub platform.

Every conclusion,

finding,

recommendation,

decision,

and compliance result

must be supported

by verifiable evidence.

Evidence is the foundation

of every architecture review.

Without evidence

there is no conclusion.

---

# Mission

Ensure every review

is

Objective

Repeatable

Auditable

Traceable

Evidence-Based

Transparent

Deterministic

---

# Core Principles

Evidence

always overrides opinion.

Evidence

always overrides assumptions.

Evidence

must be reproducible.

Evidence

must be traceable.

Evidence

must be verifiable.

Evidence

must never be fabricated.

---

# Evidence Definition

Evidence

is any verifiable artifact

that supports

or disproves

a statement.

Evidence

must exist

before

a conclusion

can be made.

# Evidence Validation

Every evidence item

must answer

What is being verified?

Where is the source?

Can it be reproduced?

Can it be independently verified?

Does it support the conclusion?

---

# Invalid Evidence

The following

are never

acceptable evidence

Assumptions

Guesses

Opinions

Speculation

Estimated behavior

Expected implementation

Future work

Verbal statements

Undocumented comments

---

# Missing Evidence

If evidence

cannot be located

Output

NOT FOUND

Do not

guess.

Do not

assume.

Do not

invent.

Do not

infer.
