## Principle 2

---

## Principle 4

to

lower authority.

---

## Principle 5

are prohibited.

---

## Principle 6

The validator

---

## Principle 7

block

Documentation Freeze.

---

## Principle 8

One dependency

must have

one authoritative target.

for the same dependency

are prohibited.

---

## Principle 9

must be deterministic.

The same dependency graph

must always produce

the same validation result.

---

## Principle 10

Every dependency

must be traceable

to

an approved engineering source.
