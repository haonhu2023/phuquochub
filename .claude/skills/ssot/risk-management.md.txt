# Purpose

This document defines

the official Risk Management Policy

for

the PhuQuocHub platform.

Risk Management

ensures

all technical,

architectural,

operational,

security,

performance,

and business risks

are identified,

evaluated,

mitigated,

tracked,

and continuously monitored

throughout

the software lifecycle.

# Mission

Create

a proactive

risk management framework

that minimizes

project failure,

production incidents,

security vulnerabilities,

architecture erosion,

schedule delays,

and technical debt.
