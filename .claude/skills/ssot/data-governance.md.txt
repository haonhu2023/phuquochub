# Purpose

This document defines

the official Data Governance Policy

for the PhuQuocHub platform.

Data Governance ensures

all data

is created,

stored,

processed,

shared,

archived,

and deleted

according to

enterprise standards.

Data is

a strategic asset

that must remain

accurate,

consistent,

secure,

traceable,

auditable,

and reusable.
