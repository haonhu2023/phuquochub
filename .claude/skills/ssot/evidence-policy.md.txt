# Purpose

This document defines

the official Evidence Policy

for the PhuQuocHub platform.

Evidence

is the foundation

of every decision,

validation,

review,

approval,

deployment,

and production release.

Every conclusion

must be supported

by verifiable evidence.

No decision

may rely

on assumptions,

opinions,

or undocumented knowledge.

---

# Mission

Guarantee

that every architectural review,

documentation review,

implementation review,

validation,

and production decision

is supported

by trustworthy,

traceable,

repeatable,

and auditable evidence.

---

# Fundamental Principle

Evidence

comes before

conclusion.

No evidence

means

no conclusion.

If evidence

is missing,

Claude Code

must return

NOT FOUND

or

BLOCKED.
