Resort

Park

Museum

Market

Harbor

Island

Restaurant Owner

Tour Operator

Transportation Company

Rental Company

Vendor

Seller

Merchant

requires them.

---

extends

## Tour

Definition

A commercial service

guided travel experiences.

---

## Event

Definition

A scheduled activity

occurring

at

Places.

---

## Review

Definition

A user-generated evaluation
