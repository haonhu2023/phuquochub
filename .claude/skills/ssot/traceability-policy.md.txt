# Purpose

This document defines

the official Traceability Policy

for the PhuQuocHub platform.

Traceability

ensures

every artifact

can be traced

from

its business origin

to

its production implementation.

Every requirement,

decision,

document,

task,

source file,

test,

deployment,

and production release

must participate

in

one continuous

traceability chain.

---

# Mission

Provide

complete visibility

into

how

business requirements

become

production software,

ensuring

every engineering activity

is

traceable,

auditable,

verifiable,

and reproducible.

---

# Fundamental Principle

Nothing

exists

without origin.

Nothing

is implemented

without approval.

Nothing

is deployed

without traceability.

Every artifact

must have

an ancestor

and

a descendant.
